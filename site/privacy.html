Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short

Due Date Check runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform and qualifies for Atlassian's "Runs on Atlassian" programme. It has no servers of its own and sends nothing outside Atlassian.

What it reads

Ticket key, summary, work type, labels, components, status, created, resolved and due dates, for the project you are viewing. It reads them as you, with your Jira permissions, so it never sees anything you can't.

What it stores

Nothing. Numbers are worked out fresh each time and are not saved. The panel keeps your last date change in your own browser for five minutes so you can Undo it.

What it changes

Only a ticket's due date, and only when you press "Use the realistic date" and confirm.

Rovo

When you use Due Date Coach, your question and the app's answer are processed by Atlassian Rovo under Atlassian's own terms.
