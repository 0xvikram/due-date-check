Support

Something not working, or a question about a number? Email vikrams.builds@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

Common fixes

  • "Needs at least 3 finished tickets": the project doesn't have enough resolved tickets yet. It works as soon as it does.
  • Project page says "Nothing to measure yet": none of the finished tickets had a due date. Ticket estimates still work.
  • A number looks wrong: click the tickets listed under it. They are exactly the ones used. Tickets with odd created or resolved dates (for example, imported in bulk) can skew results.
